Do Meal Kits Save Money? If you’ve ever stared down a grocery receipt and thought, "I spent how much for three dinners and a bunch of wilted kale?", you’re not alone. Meal kits, prepared meal options, and delivery services are all trying to answer that same modern question: how do we eat well, save time, and keep our budgets in check?
